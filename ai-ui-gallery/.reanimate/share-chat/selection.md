# share-chat: selection

Candidates reviewed (one screenshot and one 3 s capture each):
- UIStacks "Share chat" (https://uistacks.dev/components/share-chat): loaded. A share card with Only me / Workspace / Anyone segments and a sliding pill, a link field whose icon, address and Copy button morph with the choice, a Copied state with drawn check, a link-expiry menu, an Include files switch, a preview of the first two messages (dims when sharing is off), and a self-playing 12 s demo loop.
- assistant-ui "Shared conversation" (https://www.assistant-ui.com/elements/shared-conversation): loaded. A clean read-only transcript card with a "Continue in your own chat" button and variants (No turns, Question only, Long thread). Static; no motion of its own.

Winner: UIStacks Share chat. It is more polished and more complete (visibility states, copy feedback, expiry, attachments, preview) and has far richer motion. assistant-ui's viewer is a good but different, smaller pattern (the read-only recipient view), so no second clone.
Fidelity: motion-faithful (UIStacks is paid and its license could not be verified). Own palette, Geist, lucide icons, fictional names.
